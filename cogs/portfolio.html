AstraThought — local AI task classifier

Python · SetFit (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) · PyTorch · runs locally on a GTX 1070 host

Small local classifier that decides whether a Discord message is a task. I trained the model myself. All 33 of 33 commits in the repo are mine. Training data: 1,911 hand-labelled rows plus 1,000 synthetic rows; retraining is batched at 50 new human labels. Runs as a loopback-only service; a Discord cog posts triage embeds with approve/disagree buttons, and rulings feed back as training labels.

Final figures, both at the 0.5 line:

Test setPiecesPrecisionRecall
Real #astra-feedback (human rulings)6098% (46 of 47)92% (46 of 50)
Stress set (reviewed by me)10094% (51 of 54)98% (51 of 52)

The real set is the headline — 60 messages from the channel with human rulings. The stress set is 100 AI-written rows I reviewed, a harder test of what the model rejects. Earlier held-out test (test.jsonl, 86 rows, model v2): 77% precision / 93% recall.

github.com/theruvial/AstraThought — public: code, tests and docs. The training data and the model stay on my machine because they come from real team messages.
